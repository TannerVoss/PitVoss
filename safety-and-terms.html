Attend your grill and follow the appliance manufacturer's instructions. Use controls only when you can verify the appliance is in a safe condition. Check the appliance panel after changing power or temperature. A command acknowledgment is not proof that the appliance acted on it.

Do not use the app as a fire alarm, a guaranteed temperature alarm, a food-safety measurement, or a substitute for a suitable thermometer and safe cooking practices. Readings may be stale or unavailable. Notifications and stop commands may fail if the app stops, Android restricts it, connectivity is lost, or the appliance loses power.

Remote priming is unavailable in the general-release build because an app-side timer cannot guarantee that a stop command reaches the appliance. Experimental builds may expose it only for attended testing. The app never claims that closing it turns off your grill.

Use the app only with devices and networks you own or are authorized to use. Do not expose a grill's local interface to the public internet. Selecting another model does not change the hardware or make unsupported features safe or functional.
