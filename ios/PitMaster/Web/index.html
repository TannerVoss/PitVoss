About & Help

Pit Master Controller

Independent grill companion. Free and ad-free. Model compatibility is experimental; a listed model is not a claim of hardware testing.

Connection help

Keep your phone near the grill and on the same home network. Allow Bluetooth and Local Network access in iPhone Settings. Enter your Wi-Fi network name manually; iOS does not offer a general nearby-network scan.

Some firmware has no local Wi-Fi interface. Bluetooth-only controls and password-protected firmware are not yet implemented. Live temperature alerts and timers require this iPhone app to remain open. Alerts and live readings pause while the app is suspended.

If setup fails, open My Grills → Connections → Share Test Report. Review the report before sharing. Never include your Wi-Fi password.

On restricted or guest Wi-Fi, devices may be unable to reach each other. The app only connects to private local IPv4 addresses. Remote priming is disabled in the general-release build.

Your data

Saved grills, cook history, timers, preferences and diagnostic logs stay on this device unless you choose to export or share them.

Backups contain saved names, network addresses and cook data. Store them privately.

This does not erase network settings on the grill or previously exported files.
