1. Scope

This policy describes the Android Pit Master Controller source currently published in the project repository (the “App”) and how it handles information when connecting to and controlling compatible grills. Other development versions may have different features; their practices must be checked before release. The App is independently developed and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pit Boss, Dansons, or any appliance manufacturer.

2. Information handled by the App

The current version does not require user accounts and does not intentionally include advertising, analytics SDKs, or automatic developer-operated crash reporting. The App handles the following information to provide requested features:

  • Device and network discovery: Nearby Bluetooth device names, identifiers, advertised services, signal strengths and related discovery information, plus local-network addresses used to find the grill. Android may require Nearby Devices and/or Location permissions and enabled Location services for Bluetooth discovery. This Android snapshot does not provide a nearby Wi-Fi network-name scanner. The App does not intentionally request GPS coordinates or collect a location history, and does not request background location permission.
  • Locally stored information: Grill names, selected profiles, local network addresses, cooking history, timers, alert thresholds, settings, monitoring preferences, and a bounded number of diagnostic-log entries. These are stored on the user's device.
  • Wi-Fi credentials and controller responses: This Android snapshot does not offer a Wi-Fi password provisioning form. It communicates with a grill already configured for network access. Responses received from some controllers may contain device or network-related information, potentially including controller credentials. The App processes controller responses in memory, and diagnostic output can contain controller response content. Do not assume diagnostics are free of secrets.
  • Background operation: If enabled, monitoring uses local connectivity and a visible ongoing notification. Android may restrict or terminate background activity. Notifications are not guaranteed to arrive.

Local communication with a grill may expose network traffic to other parties who have access to the same network. Certain compatible grills use unencrypted HTTP and device-specific Bluetooth provisioning methods. Do not treat those channels as confidential or tamper-proof.

3. How information is used

Information is processed to discover and connect to grills, display readings, send user-requested commands, operate timers and notifications, retain local history and preferences, and troubleshoot problems when users explicitly share reports. The publisher does not operate a cloud service for routine grill operation in this version.

4. Sharing and disclosures

The App does not intentionally send stored cooking data or diagnostics to the publisher automatically. When you export cook history or choose to copy or share a Bluetooth diagnostic log, the selected information is transferred to the clipboard, file, application or destination you choose. History exports may contain cook names, timestamps and temperature readings. Diagnostic logs may include device identifiers, local IP addresses, controller responses and connection information. The Android source currently published in this repository does not automatically redact these exports. Review and remove private information, including any credentials, before sharing. Messaging, email, file-storage and other destinations have their own privacy practices.

If you contact support, Tanner Voss may receive your email address, message, attachments, and other information you voluntarily provide. Such information is used to answer requests, investigate problems, maintain support records, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Support messages are processed by the user's and publisher's email providers, under their applicable terms. The publisher may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety, to the extent permitted by law.

5. Retention and deletion

App data remains locally stored until removed through Android’s clear-storage controls or by uninstalling, subject to operating-system behavior. This Android snapshot does not include an in-app Delete Local Data feature. The App is configured not to participate in Android backup, but the publisher cannot control independently created device copies, exported files, or information retained on the grill. Deleting App data does not remove information already exported, emailed to support, or stored by the grill.

Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary for support, security, recordkeeping, and legal compliance. Users may request deletion of support information by emailing the contact above; some information may need to be retained where lawfully required or permitted. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

6. Permissions and user choices

Users may revoke permissions in Android Settings, disable optional monitoring, clear app storage in Android Settings, and stop using the App. Revoking permissions may disable features that depend on them. Location-related Android permissions used for Bluetooth discovery do not mean the App intentionally collects GPS location history.

7. Security

The App uses device-local storage and is intended to communicate with a grill on a trusted local network. The Android source currently published in this repository does not enforce a private-address-only allowlist or explicitly disable HTTP redirects. Do not enter public addresses or rely on the App to enforce that boundary. Nevertheless, no software, wireless connection, or local network is guaranteed secure. Legacy grill communication may be unencrypted, and the publisher cannot guarantee the security of appliance firmware, Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth implementations, or devices controlled by third parties. Use a trusted private network; never expose the grill's control interface directly to the public internet.

8. Children

The App is intended for adults operating cooking appliances and is not directed to children. The publisher does not knowingly solicit personal information from children. Contact the publisher if you believe a child has provided personal information directly to support.

9. Privacy rights

Depending on where users live, applicable laws may provide rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other controls over personal information held by the publisher. Contact the address above to submit a request. Information held only on the user's device generally must be managed on that device. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot legally be waived.

10. Changes

This policy may change when features, technology, or data practices change. The updated version will state its effective date and be made available through the App or its published policy page. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. Where legally required, consent will be obtained before new processing occurs.
