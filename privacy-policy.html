Pit Master Controller — Privacy Policy

Pit Master Controller is an independent app for communicating with compatible grills. This version is free and contains no ads, accounts, analytics SDKs or automatic crash-report uploads.

The app accesses nearby Bluetooth devices and, when you request a Wi-Fi search, nearby Wi-Fi network names. Android may require precise Location permission and Location services for Wi-Fi scanning. The app does not request GPS coordinates or track your location. It does not request background location access.

Saved grill names, selected models, local network addresses, cook history, timers, alert limits, preferences and bounded diagnostic logs are stored on your device. Monitoring preferences are also stored locally so optional background notifications can run. Monitoring is optional, uses a visible ongoing notification, and may be interrupted by Android, network loss or power loss.

A Wi-Fi password you enter is sent to the grill you connect to for setup. The app does not intentionally save that password or send it to the developer. The app clears the password field after submission or closing setup. The grill and Android manage their own stored network settings; removing a grill from this app does not erase settings on the grill.

Compatible legacy grills communicate over unencrypted HTTP on your private local network. Bluetooth provisioning security depends on the grill and its firmware. Use a trusted network and connect only to your own appliance. Native grill HTTP requests reject public internet addresses and redirects. The app does not operate a developer cloud service.

If you choose Share Test Report or export cook history, Android's share sheet or your selected destination receives the information you choose to share. Reports include model, controller type, connection status, selected readings, recent actions and sanitized diagnostics. Network addresses are redacted by default. Review the report before sending it; anything you add may identify you. Information you independently send to support is used to respond and troubleshoot; the provider you use to send it has its own privacy practices.

You can delete locally stored app data in About & Help → Delete Local Data, or by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings. Uninstalling removes the app's local data, subject to Android behavior; backup is disabled for this app. Exported files, messages you already sent and settings stored on the grill are not deleted by these actions. You can revoke permissions in Android Settings at any time.

The app is intended for adult appliance operators, not directed to children. It is provided in English. Applicable privacy and consumer rights are not limited by this policy. This policy must be updated if data practices change.
